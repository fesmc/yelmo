## Vertical velocity (`uz_method = 4`)

Design and results: [../uz-flux-consistent.md](../uz-flux-consistent.md); physics: docs/physics/vertical-velocity.md.

- Layer mass budget, closed against the applied transport `dHidt_dyn` where `mask_kin = 1`. Surface kinematic condition at round-off (A4, ISLAND4, TROUGH); A4 works with one row per parameter set.
- The 0.2 m a⁻¹ Greenland "residual" was an analysis artifact: output and restart hold averaged `mb_relax`/`smb` (`calc_ytopo_rates`, `overwrite`), `uz_srf_err` the last instantaneous step. In-code closure is exact.
- Effect: less downward w* where method 3 has a large negative mismatch (margins, fast flow) → warmer base. 1-kyr initmip (Levante, frz-scale defaults): grounded `f_pmp` +0.04–0.05, grounded speed +20–25 %, volume −0.5 %, worse fit to observations (friction not re-optimised).
- The w terms in the strain rate are negligible (12 runs on Levante, `output/uzw1k`).
- Regression and EISMINT symmetry pass (EXPA margin ~2× instead of ~13×). MISMIP+ grounding line one cell downstream.
- Open: switch of the default to 4 (not decided).

Spawned as a separate session ("calving of thin unconfined floating front cells").

## ISLAND4 8 km: cost of method 4

C0 at 8 km took 3.8 h with method 3 and 8 h with method 4 (Levante, 16 threads). Swap test on albedo (`output/uzdt8`): 500 yr from the final C0 state of each method, run with each method, `log_timestep = True`.

| state / method | Picard iterations per step | max velocity change per step (median) | kyr/hr |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 / 3 | 2.3 | 0.05 m a⁻¹ | 18.8 |
| 3 / 4 | 9.4 (rises from 2 to 13 within ~100 yr) | 0.3 m a⁻¹ | 8.3 |
| 4 / 3 | 8.5 | 1.4 m a⁻¹ | 6.8 |
| 4 / 4 | 9.2 | 1.9 m a⁻¹ | 5.8 |

- The time step is the same in all runs (3.3 yr); the cost is the number of SSA Picard iterations at the tight ISLAND4 tolerance (1e-5).
- The method-4 state is not steady. The grounded strip on the trough flanks (r ≈ 385–400 km, bed −100 to −260 m, temperate base) oscillates with a period of ~350 yr: till water falls from 2.0 to 1.2 m, beta rises ~10×, sliding drops from 90 to 13 m a⁻¹ and the ice thickens by ~200 m; then the till refills, beta drops ~20× and the strip surges (150–180 m a⁻¹) and thins. The neighbouring shelf follows. The velocity changes everywhere, so each solve needs more iterations. In the method-3 state the strip is thick (570–850 m), saturated and static.
- Method 3 run from the method-4 state stops the oscillation within ~300 yr; after 2 kyr the iterations are back to 2.5 per step (`s4-m3-2k`). Method 4 sustains it.
- The volume drift criterion does not detect it: V is steady while the margin oscillates. C0 needs a criterion on velocity or grounded area as well.
- Not all method-4 states oscillate: C0 at 32 and 16 km and the 16 km sweep with D0 = 1000 m are steady; the 16 km sweep run with D0 = 700 m, r_ela = 650 km oscillates (grounded speed ±11 m a⁻¹). The 8 km C0 with the new geometry shows no oscillation up to 26 kyr.
- Open: physical (thermal binge-purge of a thin temperate grounded strip) or a method-4 artifact at the grounding line. Test running: D0 = 700 m, r_ela = 650 km at 16 km with method 3 (`output/c0sweep/d700-ela650-z200-uz3`). If method 3 is steady there, the next step is to log basal melt and the method-4 correction `D_corr` at the flank cells.
- This is the condition for switching the default to method 4.

## ISLAND4 C0

Runs: Levante `~/models/yelmo/output/c0/island4-{32,16,8}km-uz{3,4}` (50 kyr, 2D every 1 kyr, restarts every 10 kyr); local copies `output/levante/c0/`. Fixtures from the current Julia defaults (`r_ela` 650 km, Ω = 0.01, z0 = −200 m), `init = vialov_grounded`.

```bash
runme -s -r -q shared -w 24:00:00 -e bench -n par/yelmo_bench_ISLAND4.nml -o output/c0/island4-8km-uz4 -p bench.fixture=input/bench/island4-8km.nc yelmo.grid_name=ISLAND4-8KM ydyn.uz_method=4 ctrl.time_end=50e3 ctrl.dt2D_out=1000 ctrl.dt_restart=10e3
```

| V [10⁶ km³] / A_g [10⁶ km²] | 32 km | 16 km | 8 km |
|---|---|---|---|
| method 3 | 1.28 / 0.89 | 0.94 / 0.71 | 0.76 / 0.58 |
| method 4 | 0.95 / 0.73 | 0.76 / 0.59 | 0.72 / 0.55 |

- All runs meet the drift criterion (|dV/dt|/V < 0.1 % kyr⁻¹, agreed) after ~35 kyr.
- Run time (16 threads): 32 km 14–32 min, 16 km ~70 min, 8 km 3.8 h (method 3) and 8 h (method 4).
- The state converges with resolution, method 4 faster (16 km method 4 ≈ 8 km method 3), towards a grounding line at the coastline everywhere: the grounded marine ice of the diagonal sectors at 32/16 km is a resolution artifact, lost in one-cell retreat steps.
- Consequence: the converged C0 has no grounding line on a marine bed; it is pinned by the coastal bed step, with unconfined shelves. This contradicts the intended ISLAND4 design (grounded ice below sea level, grounding line, embayment shelves).

## Decision pending

1. Accept the coastal grounding line as the C0 reference; leave marine grounding-line migration to the B tier.
2. Retune ISLAND4 for marine-grounded ice in the diagonal sectors (larger `r_ela`, shallower marine bed `Bl`, deeper melt depth `z0`): sweep at 16 km with method 4 (closest cheap proxy for the converged state), then confirm at 8 km. The earlier `Bl` sweep was at 32 km, which overstates marine grounded ice.

Recommended: option 2.

Decided (2026-10-07): option 2. Sweep at 16 km, method 4 (albedo `output/c0sweep`, D0 × r_ela × z0): D0 controls the grounding line, Bl and z0 are weak. New defaults D0 = 1000 m, r_ela = 800 km (grounding line on the trough floor at r ≈ 465 km). Details in index.md.
